Recommended Route

Story Focus

Perfect for first-time overseas players. Prioritize main quests, collect waypoints, basic resources, and light side quests along the way.

  1. Progress main quest until core systems unlock
  2. Unlock waypoints in each new area first
  3. Only take short-chain side quests
