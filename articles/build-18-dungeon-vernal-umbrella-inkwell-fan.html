Dungeon Vernal Umbrella + Inkwell Fan Build Guide

English build guide for Dungeon Vernal Umbrella + Inkwell Fan: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Intermediate6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Dungeon Vernal Umbrella + Inkwell Fan is best treated as a Ranged DPS / PvE. Prioritize ATK > Crit Rate > Range, then test it in Dungeons and Weekly bosses before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Umbrella and Fan as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Pre-buff before contact; Build resource on primary weapon; Swap for break or crowd-control window; Use burst after teammate setup.
  3. Use heart methods such as Flower Moon, Yi Shui Song, Star Pluck when available.
  4. Tune stats toward ATK > Crit Rate > Range rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

Dungeon route tuned for stable damage windows, resource efficiency, and repeatable boss clears. Aerial control with safe ranged pressure. Its PvE rating is 9/10 and PvP rating is 8/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Umbrella + Fan
  • Skills: Green Mountain + Nine Springs
  • Stats: ATK > Crit Rate > Range
  • Heart methods: Flower Moon, Yi Shui Song, Star Pluck
  • Use cases: Dungeons, Weekly bosses, Material farming
