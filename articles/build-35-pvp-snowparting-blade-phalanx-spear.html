PvP Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear Build Guide

English build guide for PvP Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Advanced6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

PvP Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear is best treated as a Boss Breaker / Duel. Prioritize Break > ATK > Crit Rate, then test it in Duel and Small-scale PvP before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Blade and Spear as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Test spacing with low-commitment poke; Hold mobility until enemy commit; Punish whiff with secondary weapon; Disengage before stamina is empty.
  3. Use heart methods such as Cold Ridge, Yi Shui Song, Battle Standard when available.
  4. Tune stats toward Break > ATK > Crit Rate rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

PvP route focused on mobility, punish timing, and short confirmed damage windows. Designed around stance break and safe punish windows. Its PvE rating is 8/10 and PvP rating is 7/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Blade + Spear
  • Skills: Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear
  • Stats: Break > ATK > Crit Rate
  • Heart methods: Cold Ridge, Yi Shui Song, Battle Standard
  • Use cases: Duel, Small-scale PvP, Counter-pick practice
