Qinghe Hidden Entrance Notes

Qinghe Hidden Entrance Notes for Where Winds Meet exploration planning.

All players6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Treat hidden entrances as optional notes until mobility and nearby waypoints are ready.

Recommended Steps

  1. Open the closest waypoint before starting the route.
  2. Clear nearby rewards in a tight circle.
  3. Mark bosses, hidden entrances, and puzzle starts separately.
  4. Return to hub when inventory or upgrades interrupt the route.

Why This Works

The game is built around a large open world with many regions and live updates. Clear map notes make new regions easier to fold into daily play without losing older progress.

Common Mistakes

  • Walking long routes before opening fast travel.
  • Mixing verified points with rumors in the same list.
  • Treating expansion zones like finished static maps immediately after a patch.

Practical Checklist

  • Waypoint opened
  • Nearby rewards checked
  • Boss or event marker recorded
  • Source note added
  • Next route chosen

This guide avoids unverified coordinates or drop rates. Patch-sensitive details should be rechecked after major updates.
