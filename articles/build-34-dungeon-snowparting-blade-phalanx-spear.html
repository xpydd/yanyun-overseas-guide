Dungeon Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear Build Guide

English build guide for Dungeon Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Intermediate6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Dungeon Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear is best treated as a Boss Breaker / PvE. Prioritize Break > ATK > Crit Rate, then test it in Dungeons and Weekly bosses before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Blade and Spear as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Pre-buff before contact; Build resource on primary weapon; Swap for break or crowd-control window; Use burst after teammate setup.
  3. Use heart methods such as Cold Ridge, Yi Shui Song, Battle Standard when available.
  4. Tune stats toward Break > ATK > Crit Rate rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

Dungeon route tuned for stable damage windows, resource efficiency, and repeatable boss clears. Designed around stance break and safe punish windows. Its PvE rating is 10/10 and PvP rating is 6/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Blade + Spear
  • Skills: Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear
  • Stats: Break > ATK > Crit Rate
  • Heart methods: Cold Ridge, Yi Shui Song, Battle Standard
  • Use cases: Dungeons, Weekly bosses, Material farming
