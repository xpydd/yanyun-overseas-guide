Starter Vernal Umbrella + Inkwell Fan Build Guide

English build guide for Starter Vernal Umbrella + Inkwell Fan: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Easy6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Starter Vernal Umbrella + Inkwell Fan is best treated as a Ranged DPS / Training. Prioritize ATK > Crit Rate > Range, then test it in First week progression and Solo story before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Umbrella and Fan as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Open with safe ranged or poke skill; Use primary weapon for steady damage; Swap only after dodge or break window; Spend burst when boss recovery is visible.
  3. Use heart methods such as Flower Moon, Yi Shui Song, Star Pluck when available.
  4. Tune stats toward ATK > Crit Rate > Range rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

Beginner-friendly route that keeps the core weapon pair intact while lowering execution risk. Aerial control with safe ranged pressure. Its PvE rating is 8/10 and PvP rating is 7/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Umbrella + Fan
  • Skills: Green Mountain + Nine Springs
  • Stats: ATK > Crit Rate > Range
  • Heart methods: Flower Moon, Yi Shui Song, Star Pluck
  • Use cases: First week progression, Solo story, Learning boss patterns
