Dungeon Panacea Fan + Soulshade Umbrella Build Guide

English build guide for Dungeon Panacea Fan + Soulshade Umbrella: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Easy6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Dungeon Panacea Fan + Soulshade Umbrella is best treated as a Support/Healer / PvE. Prioritize Healing > HP > DEF, then test it in Dungeons and Weekly bosses before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Fan and Umbrella as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Pre-buff before contact; Build resource on primary weapon; Swap for break or crowd-control window; Use burst after teammate setup.
  3. Use heart methods such as Medicine King, Apricot Blossom, Silk Thread when available.
  4. Tune stats toward Healing > HP > DEF rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

Dungeon route tuned for stable damage windows, resource efficiency, and repeatable boss clears. Team sustain with shields and safe ranged uptime. Its PvE rating is 9/10 and PvP rating is 7/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Fan + Umbrella
  • Skills: Medicine Canon + Soul Shade
  • Stats: Healing > HP > DEF
  • Heart methods: Medicine King, Apricot Blossom, Silk Thread
  • Use cases: Dungeons, Weekly bosses, Material farming
