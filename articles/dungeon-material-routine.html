Dungeon Material Routine

Dungeon Material Routine for Where Winds Meet overseas players.

All players6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Run dungeons with a target material in mind, not just because the daily checklist says dungeon.

Recommended Steps

  1. Check announcements and mail.
  2. Run the quick daily list.
  3. Spend stamina on current build goals.
  4. Save one note about tomorrow's target.

Why This Works

Daily consistency matters more than oversized plans. A small routine protects progress while leaving time for story, map, or PvP when you actually have energy.

Common Mistakes

  • Letting stamina cap while chasing optional map cleanup.
  • Doing PvP after you are already tired.
  • Forgetting to update the routine after a region or event patch.

Practical Checklist

  • Check announcements and claim mail rewards (2 min)
  • Complete daily quests (3-5 tasks) (15 min)
  • Run one dungeon for materials (10 min)
  • Spend stamina on resource nodes (5 min)
  • Quick guild check-in (1 min)
  • Claim idle rewards (2 min)

Sources And Notes

This guide avoids unverified coordinates or drop rates. Patch-sensitive details should be rechecked after major updates.
