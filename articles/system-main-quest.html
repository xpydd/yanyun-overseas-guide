Quick Answer

Main Quest (主线任务) is part of the quest vocabulary players need when following English and Chinese guides.

Recommended Steps

Why This Works

Terminology gaps are one of the fastest ways to misread a build, quest, or system guide. A small term reference reduces that friction.
