Starter Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear Build Guide

English build guide for Starter Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Easy6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Starter Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear is best treated as a Boss Breaker / Training. Prioritize Break > ATK > Crit Rate, then test it in First week progression and Solo story before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Blade and Spear as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Open with safe ranged or poke skill; Use primary weapon for steady damage; Swap only after dodge or break window; Spend burst when boss recovery is visible.
  3. Use heart methods such as Cold Ridge, Yi Shui Song, Battle Standard when available.
  4. Tune stats toward Break > ATK > Crit Rate rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

Beginner-friendly route that keeps the core weapon pair intact while lowering execution risk. Designed around stance break and safe punish windows. Its PvE rating is 9/10 and PvP rating is 5/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Blade + Spear
  • Skills: Snowparting Blade + Phalanx Spear
  • Stats: Break > ATK > Crit Rate
  • Heart methods: Cold Ridge, Yi Shui Song, Battle Standard
  • Use cases: First week progression, Solo story, Learning boss patterns
