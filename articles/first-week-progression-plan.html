First Week Progression Plan

First Week Progression Plan for Where Winds Meet overseas players.

All players6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Use a low-risk weekly path: main quest, waypoints, daily checklist, then build refinement.

Recommended Steps

  1. Confirm your platform and account route before investing time.
  2. Unlock main systems and nearby waypoints first.
  3. Use the 45-minute checklist until your build direction is stable.
  4. Record terms or locations that do not match your UI language.

Why This Works

Where Winds Meet mixes open-world exploration, martial arts builds, and live updates. A stable onboarding routine keeps you from wasting early resources or following outdated advice.

Common Mistakes

  • Starting collectible cleanup before waypoints are open.
  • Changing servers after friends have already committed.
  • Copying a high execution build before learning dodge and parry timing.

Practical Checklist

  • Account route confirmed
  • Server region chosen
  • First waypoint loop started
  • Daily checklist saved
  • Starter build selected
