Farm Bellstrike Umbra + Ten Directions Build Guide

English build guide for Farm Bellstrike Umbra + Ten Directions: role, weapons, stat priority, skill loop, replacements, and use cases.

Easy6 min readUpdated May 12, 2026

Quick Answer

Farm Bellstrike Umbra + Ten Directions is best treated as a Bruiser / Resource. Prioritize ATK > Crit > Penetration, then test it in Daily checklist and Open-world farming before committing rare upgrades.

Recommended Steps

  1. Equip Blade and Mo Blade as the core weapon pair.
  2. Practice this loop: Group minor enemies first; Use area skill to clean up; Reserve mobility for travel; Heal or reset before the next pack.
  3. Use heart methods such as Frost Night, Yi Shui Song, Throat Pierce when available.
  4. Tune stats toward ATK > Crit > Penetration rather than chasing every shiny upgrade.

Why This Works

Low-maintenance route for daily farming, open-world errands, and fragmented play sessions. Flexible blade style with strong stagger control. Its PvE rating is 8/10 and PvP rating is 6/10 in this site's internal route model, so it should be tested in the content type you actually play.

Common Mistakes

  • Upgrading both weapons evenly before knowing the main damage window.
  • Ignoring defensive replacements when learning bosses.
  • Using a PvP variant for long PvE farming without adjusting sustain.

Practical Checklist

  • Weapons: Blade + Mo Blade
  • Skills: Snow Cut + Ten Directions
  • Stats: ATK > Crit > Penetration
  • Heart methods: Frost Night, Yi Shui Song, Throat Pierce
  • Use cases: Daily checklist, Open-world farming, Short sessions
